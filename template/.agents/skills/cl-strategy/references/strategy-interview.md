# Strategy interview

Ask one substantive question at a time.

## Target problem

Ask: What repeated, costly problem is this product or Agent solving?

Push back when:

- The answer is a vague market category.
- The problem is actually a proposed feature.
- No real user behavior or cost is named.

## Approach

Ask: What is the guiding approach or thesis?

Push back when:

- The answer is a list of features.
- The answer could describe any competitor.
- There is no choice or tradeoff.

## Primary users

Ask: Who is the first narrow user segment?

Push back when:

- The answer is everyone.
- The segment is too broad to design for.

## Key metrics

Ask: What signals show users received value?

Push back when:

- The metric is vanity-only.
- The metric cannot be measured.
- The metric is not connected to user value.

## Tracks

Ask: What 1-3 work tracks matter now?

Push back when:

- There are more than three active tracks.
- A track is a single feature, not a sustained capability area.

## Not working on

Ask: What tempting work is intentionally out of scope?

## Agent-native assumptions

Ask: For Agent behavior, what context, tools, permissions, memory, and evals define success?
